## Design Context

### Users
People who already run Grok Bots and are tired of every bot sharing one machine. They arrive curious, not to configure infrastructure.

### Brand Personality
Editorial, dry, exact. Cream paper on charcoal. Lime only when something is live.

### Aesthetic Direction
Instrument Serif + IBM Plex Sans. Cream `#f2e6d0`, ink `#23190f`, lime `#b7e04a`, amber `#b5471b`. Oval eyes on a color tile. Not Inter, not indigo, not generic SaaS cards, not VNC chrome.

### Design Principles
1. Five seconds to understand: bot, own computer.
2. One primary action per page.
3. Fail closed in copy as well as in product.
4. Screenshot of the desk is the hero, not an icon grid.
5. 390px first.
